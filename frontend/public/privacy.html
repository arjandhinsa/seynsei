Privacy Policy

Seynsei · Effective 4 July 2026

Seynsei is a social confidence practice app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything yourself at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and an optional display name, used to create and secure your account. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes; we cannot read them.
  • Profile answers. If you choose to personalise the app: your focus area, the situations you find hardest, and how at ease you feel. These shape your coaching and recommendations. All are optional and editable.
  • Practice data. Challenges you complete, your before-and-after anxiety ratings, and any notes you write. This exists so you can see your own progress.
  • Coach conversations. Messages you exchange with Sensei, our AI coach, so your conversation history works.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

To run the service: sign you in, save your progress, personalise your challenges, and power your conversations with Sensei. That's the whole list. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not show ads.

AI processing

Messages you send to Sensei (and limited context, such as your profile answers and the challenge you're working on) are processed by OpenAI's API to generate replies. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in managed databases operated by our hosting providers, encrypted in transit. Access is limited to what is needed to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account, in the app) permanently removes your account and all associated data — completions, ratings, notes, conversations, and profile — immediately and irreversibly. You can also email us to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under data protection law including access, correction, deletion, and portability of your personal data. Contact us and we'll help.

A note on wellbeing

Seynsei offers confidence practice informed by cognitive behavioural principles. It is not therapy, medical care, or a crisis service, and it is not a substitute for professional support.

Children

Seynsei is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note the new effective date here and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
