## Description
Social confidence isn't a personality trait. It's a practice.

Seynsei gives you small, real-world challenges built on the way confidence is actually trained: gradual, repeated, real. Start with three seconds of eye contact. Work up, at your own pace, to the conversations you've been putting off.

HOW IT WORKS

• Choose a challenge that feels just slightly uncomfortable
• Rate how anxious you feel, 1 to 10, right before you start
• Do it in the real world, then rate again while it's fresh
• Watch the two numbers, side by side, tell you the truth: it comes down

Two paths, one method. The Social path covers everyday connection: ordering, phone calls, speaking up, being seen. The Dating path covers romantic confidence: showing interest, making the ask, handling silence and rejection with grace.

MEET SENSEI

A calm, CBT-informed coach in your pocket. Talk it through before a challenge when the dread is loud, or after, when you need to make sense of how it went. Sensei knows your path and meets you where you are.

BUILT TO KEEP YOU GOING

• 40 graded challenges across five tiers
• XP, streaks, and milestones that reward showing up
• A profile that reflects real progress, not vanity metrics
• Personalised to where you want to grow

Answer a few quiet questions at the start and Seynsei shapes the path around you. Browse everything before creating an account.

Seynsei is confidence practice informed by cognitive behavioural principles. It is not therapy or medical care, and it is not a substitute for professional support.

Small brave steps, daily.
